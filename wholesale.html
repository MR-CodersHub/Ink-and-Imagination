Pricing Notes

All wholesale prices are exclusive of GST (18%). Shipping costs are charged at actuals — free shipping on orders above ₹8,000. Custom/exclusive designs priced separately. Prices valid through December 2026. First-time order requires prepayment; terms available for established accounts.

How to Order

From Application to Display

📋

1. Apply

Fill in the stockist application below. We review all applications within 3 working days.

🤝

2. Welcome Call

If approved, your account manager will schedule a 20-min intro call to discuss your store and best-fit ranges.

🛒

3. Place Order

Access your retailer portal, select your mix, confirm your order. Receive trade invoice and bank details.

🚀

4. Launch!

Cards arrive beautifully packed with display materials. Set up your display and start selling within a week.
