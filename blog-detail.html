BOTANICAL CRAFT & RECIPES

The Art of Hand-Pressing Wildflowers for Deckle Cards

By Ananya Sharma • 6 min read • October 2026

There is an indelible magic in preserving a flower at the peak of its bloom. In our Jaipur paper studio, morning harvesting begins as soon as the dawn dew evaporates from our botanical garden beds.

Harvesting Flowers with High Pigment Retention

Not all flowers press equally well into cotton rag paper. Thin-petaled blooms like cosmos, bougainvillea, cornflowers, and marigolds retain vibrant pigment when pressed under heavy wooden presses for 3 to 4 weeks.

"Paper crafted by hand holds the warmth of human hands and the natural poetry of dried botanicals."

After drying, each petal is delicately set onto our wet cotton screen before the paper fibers bind together during solar drying. The result is a truly unique, one-of-a-kind piece of botanical art.
