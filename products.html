Craftsmanship

The Details That Make the Difference

📜

Cotton Rag Paper

We use only 100% cotton rag paper — the same material used for centuries in fine books and currency. It accepts ink beautifully, ages gracefully, and feels unlike anything else.

🖨️

Letterpress Printing

Our vintage Chandler & Price letterpress creates a deep, tactile impression you can feel with your fingertips. No digital printing. Just ink, pressure, and craft.

🌸

Real Pressed Botanicals

Flowers and foliage from our studio garden, pressed for weeks until perfectly flat and preserved. Each botanical piece is unique — no two cards are identical.

✨

Gold Foil Stamping

A hot-foil stamp presses real metallic foil into the card surface. Our marigold gold and rose gold foils catch light in a way that digital printing never can.

🔏

Botanical Wax Seals

Every envelope leaves our studio sealed with our signature botanical wax seal. We offer 12 seal designs, from botanicals to geometric to calligraphy initials.

📦

Gift-Ready Packaging

Each card is wrapped in tissue, placed in a kraft box tied with silk ribbon. Unboxing an Ink & Imagination card is part of the experience — before you've even read a word.
