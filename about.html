OUR STORY & CRAFTSMANSHIP

Handmaking Paper, Preserving Memories

Ink & Imagination was born out of a love for handwritten notes, slow craftsmanship, and the tactile beauty of raw deckle-edge cotton paper.

SUSTAINABLE & ARTISAN

Zero Waste, 100% Recycled Cotton Rag

We upcycle textile garment scraps from local Indian mills, breaking them down into natural cotton pulp without toxic bleaches. Every sheet is individually hand-pulled, solar-dried, and inspected for botanical beauty.
