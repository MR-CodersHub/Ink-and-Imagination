Print Techniques

Timeline

What to Expect & When

Every custom order follows a clear, transparent timeline. We keep you updated at every stage — no surprises, only beautiful results.

Day 1–2

Enquiry & Consultation

We respond within 24 hours. Schedule your free 30-min consultation call or proceed via email.

Day 3–7

Design Concept & Proof

Our designer creates your first proof. You receive a high-resolution digital mockup for review.

Day 8–10

Revisions & Approval

Two rounds of revisions included. Once you approve, we proceed to production with your payment.

Day 11–18

Crafting & Production

Cards are printed, botanicals pressed and adhered, foiled, trimmed, and quality-checked by hand.

Day 19–21

Packaging & Dispatch

Every card is individually packaged and your order dispatched via tracked courier with care instructions.
