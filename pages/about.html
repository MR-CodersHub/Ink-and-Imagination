Ananya & Twelve Pairs of Hands

Ananya Sharma started with a secondhand press in Jaipur in 2019. Today our Delhi finishing room and Jaipur paper unit employ 12 printmakers, calligraphers and flower-pressers — all trained in-house, all credited on every box.

✦ How a card is born

Four Slow Steps

  1. 01

    Pulp & Pull

    Textile cotton scraps become rainwater pulp, pulled on bamboo screens.

  2. 02

    Petal & Dry

    Pressed marigold, lavender and fern laid by tweezers, solar-dried.

  3. 03

    Press & Foil

    Heidelberg letterpress + hand-mixed inks + optional gold foil kiss.

MATERIALS

300–600 GSM, Zero Plastic

Deckle-edge cotton, soy inks, habotai silk ribbons, brass wax. Biodegradable sleeves only. Swatch kits posted before any custom run — see Service Details.
