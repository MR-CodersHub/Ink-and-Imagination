The Art of Hand-Pressing Wildflowers for Deckle Cards

By Ananya Sharma • October 2026 • Jaipur Paper Unit

There is magic in catching a flower at peak bloom. We harvest after dew lifts, press between blotters under sal-wood weights for 3–4 weeks, then lay each petal onto wet cotton pulp so fibres grip it forever.

Thin Petals Win

Cosmos, bougainvillea, cornflower and marigold keep pigment best. Thick roses brown — so we use them sparingly, as confetti flecks.
